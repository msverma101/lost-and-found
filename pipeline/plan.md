Status: draft, 15 Nov 2026. Nothing generated yet. Mirrors the canvas pages 01-05.

## 01 Brief & script
Constraints: 16:9 master 1920x1080 24fps, ~3:00, near-wordless (2 short lines + 1 VO line), plus 9:16 cutdown.
Hard no-gos: no real people's faces/voices, no brands/logos (fictional transit company "Linie Nord"), no film/artist names in prompts, no copyrighted music.

Title: LOST & FOUND
Logline: A lonely night clerk at a city lost-and-found office has kept one unclaimed music box for 40 years; when a young woman finally comes to claim it, he has to give back the last piece of the woman he loved.
Tone: tender, quiet, bittersweet. Warm sodium light vs. cold fluorescent; memories in golden 1980s film grain.

Beats (target seconds):
1 Open (0:00-0:20) last train passes, office window lit at night.
2 Establish (0:20-0:50) Otto logs forgotten things: umbrella, glove, teddy. Takes the music box from a locked drawer, winds it; warm light.
3 Turn (0:50-1:15) Memory: 1984 dance hall, young Otto and Vera dance; she leaves the box on a tram seat for him... he never found her again.
4 Escalate (1:15-1:50) Bell rings. Ines (32) at the counter with an old claim ticket, her late grandmother's handwriting. Otto hesitates, says it isn't here.
5 Peak (1:50-2:25) Ines turns to go; humming the box's melody. Otto stops her, hands it over. She opens it: a folded note inside, "For O." Vera kept it for him.
6 Resolve (2:25-2:50) Ines gives the note to Otto. He reads it; smiles. She leaves the box on the counter: "She'd want you to have it."
7 End card (2:50-3:00) Otto winds the box; melody over the empty platform. Title.

Scenes (= 4 columns on page 04): S1 office night (beats 1-2), S2 memory 1984 (3), S3 counter (4-5), S4 resolve + end (6-7). ~26 shots, 5-8 s each.
